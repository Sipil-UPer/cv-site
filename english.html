About the programme

The Civil Engineering Study Program at Universitas Pertamina is a bachelor programme of 8 semesters leading to the degree Sarjana Teknik (S.T.).

Graduate profiles

PL-1Junior Civil Engineer
PL-2Infrastructure Analyst and Planner
PL-3Construction and Infrastructure Manager
PL-4Junior Civil Engineering Researcher
PL-5Adaptive Civil Engineering Professional

Programme learning outcomes (K26)

  1. PLO AAble to demonstrate piety and integrity, and to uphold humanitarian values, nationalism, and love for the homeland.
  2. PLO BAble to act ethically, professionally, and accountably in decision-making, and to take responsibility toward oneself, society, and the organization for its impact in social, economic, environmental, and global contexts.
  3. PLO CAble to think critically, creatively, efficiently, and innovatively in identifying, formulating, analyzing, and solving complex problems in the field of study, by applying relevant principles, theories, and disciplinary methodologies and making data- and evidence-based decisions, in both the science-engineering and social-humanities domains.
  4. PLO DAble to lead and/or contribute effectively in multicultural and/or multidisciplinary teams.
  5. PLO EAble to communicate effectively, both orally and in writing, to a range of audiences.
  6. PLO FAble to develop oneself independently as a lifelong learner who keeps pace with contemporary developments.
  7. PLO GAble to apply concepts of mathematics, basic science, and engineering principles to formulate civil engineering problems into models that can be solved systematically.
  8. PLO HAble to design and conduct laboratory experiments and/or field investigations, and to analyze and interpret data to support engineering decisions.
  9. PLO IAble to design civil engineering systems, components, or processes that meet technical needs within realistic constraints, considering safety, sustainability, the environment, and energy resilience.
  10. PLO JAble to select and use appropriate modern engineering methods, tools, and technologies in civil engineering analysis and design, with an understanding of their assumptions and limitations.
  11. PLO KAble to apply principles of project management, engineering economics, and construction safety in infrastructure delivery, considering sustainability and energy resilience.

Curriculum K26

  1. Semester 117 credits

    English 1; Digital Literation; Integrative Thingking; Calculus 1; Basic Physics 1; Basic Chemistry 1; General Chemistry Laboratory; General Physics Laboratory 1

  2. Semester 219 credits

    Indonesian Language; Basic Physics 2; Calculus 2; General Physics Laboratory 2; Basic Geology; Introduction to Surveying and Mapping; Engineering Drawing; Engineering Drawing Laboratory; Introduction to Infrastructure

  3. Semester 320 credits

    Energy Literacy; Computer Programming and Applications; Statistics; Analysis of Certain Static Structures; Materials Engineering; Materials Technology Laboratory; Fluid and Hydraulic Mechanics; Fluid and Hydraulic Mechanics Laboratory; Traffic Engineering and Transportation Systems

  4. Semester 420 credits

    Civics; Differential Equation; Material Mechanics; Soil Mechanics 1; Soil Mechanics Laboratory 1; Hydrology; Road Geometry; Ocean Wave Mechanics; Construction Management

  5. Semester 519 credits

    English 2; Numerical Method; Steel Structure; Soil Mechanics 2; Soil Mechanics 2; Structural Analysis with Matrix; Concrete Structure; Engineering Economics

  6. Semester 617 credits

    Road Pavement Engineering; Road Pavement Materials Laboratory; Offshore Platform Steel Structure Design; Foundation Engineering; Earthquake Resistant Concrete Structure; Construction Implementation Methods and HSE; Dam Hydrology and Water Energy

  7. Semester 713 credits

    Innovation and Entrepreneurship; Sustainable Development; Studium Generale; Internship; Building Design and Civil Engineering Innovation; Research Methodology

  8. Semester 810 credits

    Religion and Ethics; Pancasila; Engineering Ethics; Final Project

Elective courses (21): Structural Dynamics; Offshore Platform Construction Analysis; Design of Floating Structure; Construction Management and Legal Aspects of the Project; International Standards in Construction Management; Advance Foundation Engineering; Advance Soil Mechanics; Geotechnical Earthquake Engineering; Soil Improvement and Stabilization; Transportation Modelling and Analysis; Airport Engineering; Sustainable Transportation; Advanced Hydrology; Port Engineering; Drainage Planning; Water Resources Model; Coastal Process and Structure; Coastal Planning Management; Aspects of Disaster and Infrastructure Failure; Finite Element Method; Design of Offshore Pipeline.

Course names follow the official curriculum document; courses without an English name are listed in Indonesian.

Areas of expertise

Structural Engineering and Materials

Concrete and steel structures, earthquake-resistant buildings, offshore and floating structures, construction materials

Lecturers: Arianta, Gati Annisa Hayu, Ranny Adriana

Water Resources Engineering

Hydrology and hydraulics, dams and hydropower, drainage, coastal, port, and marine engineering

Lecturers: Fera Dwi Setyani, Nurul Fajar Januriyadi, Teuku Mahlil

Transportation Engineering

Traffic and transportation systems, highway geometry and pavements, airports, sustainable transportation

Lecturers: Adita Utami, Vani Arliani

Geotechnical Engineering

Geology and soil mechanics, foundations, geotechnical earthquake engineering, ground improvement

Lecturers: Arlyn Aristo, Wirman Hidayat

Construction Engineering and Management

Project management and economics, construction methods and safety, legal aspects and international construction standards

Lecturers: M Fanshurullah Asa
